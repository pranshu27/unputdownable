## Notes
- Element/attribute vocabulary mirrors the original exactly (POWERMART 10.4, REP_DEV, INSTANCE TYPE=SOURCE/TARGET/TRANSFORMATION/MAPPLET, CONNECTOR FROM/TOPINSTANCETYPE semantics, SOURCEFIELD FIELDTYPE="E", SQ ports as TRANSFORMFIELD INPUT/OUTPUT).
- Flavor expressions are domain-specific and port-checked: any expression referencing a port its pattern doesn't carry auto-falls back to a safe generic (keeps every mapping import-valid).
- Deterministic: rerunning the generator reproduces byte-identical output.
