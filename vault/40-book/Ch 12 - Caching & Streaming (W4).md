# Ch 12 — Caching & Streaming (W4) - stub

Two ways to stop paying twice: a semantic cache that recognises when a question is a redisguised question it already answered (cosine threshold, staleness rules, and the honest danger of serving a stale answer), and streaming that makes the first token arrive before the last is computed. Both measured as hit-rate, latency delta, and tokens avoided.
