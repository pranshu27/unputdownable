# Ch 11 — Query Rewriting & Self-RAG (W3) - stub

Half of retrieval failures are corpus failures. The other half are *question* failures - ambiguous, unanswerable, badly phrased. The next chapter fixes the question before blaming the index: HyDE, sub-query decomposition, rewrite loops, and Self-RAG reflection tokens that verify the context is sufficient before the model is allowed to speak. **Written after ADR-003.**
